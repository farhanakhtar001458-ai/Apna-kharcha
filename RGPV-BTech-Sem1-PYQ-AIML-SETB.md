### 📋 Exam Pattern (Grading System - GS)
- **Time:** 3 ghante | **Max Marks:** 70 (theory) — total 150 with practical (30) + internal (20+10+20)
- **Attempt any 5 questions out of 8** — har question **14 marks** (a) + (b) = 7 + 7
- Har question alag unit se aata hai → **pura syllabus touch hota hai**, options milte hain
- Doubt hone par **English version final** maana jaata hai
- Ab RGPV exams **saal mein 2 baar** hota hai (December cycle + June cycle), isliye har subject ke multiple sets available hain 🎯

## 📝 2. FULL PAPER — December 2025 (latest Dec cycle)

### BT-201 Engineering Physics — Dec 2025
**Time: 3 hrs | Max Marks: 70 | Attempt any 5 of 8**

1. **(a)** Explain the wave nature of particles. Derive the time-independent Schrödinger equation for a particle in a one-dimensional potential box and write its wavefunction. **(b)** A particle of mass 9.11×10⁻³¹ kg is moving in a one-dimensional box of width 10 nm. Calculate the energy of the particle in the first three excited states.
2. **(a)** Describe how Newton's ring experiment can be used to determine the refractive index of a liquid. **(b)** Explain briefly the Rayleigh criterion of resolution. Discuss the resolving power of plane transmission grating and find the relation between resolving power and dispersive power of the grating.
3. **(a)** Explain Hall effect and derive expression for Hall voltage. Discuss its applications. **(b)** Explain Fermi level for intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors. Discuss the density of states and Bloch's theorem.
4. **(a)** Derive the relation between Einstein's coefficients (A & B) and explain the concept of population inversion. **(b)** Explain the construction and working of He-Ne laser with suitable diagram.
5. **(a)** Prove the Poynting theorem in electrodynamics and explain the physical significance of each term appearing in the final expression of the theorem. **(b)** Derive Maxwell's equations in vacuum.
6. **(a)** Discuss the uncertainty principle. Derive the relation between group velocity and phase velocity for a free particle. **(b)** Calculate the NA, acceptance angle, critical angle and acceptance cone for a fiber having RI of 1.55 and 1.50 respectively for its core & cladding.
7. **(a)** Explain acceptance angle and acceptance cone of optical fiber. Derive expression for them. **(b)** Discuss the phenomenon of Fraunhofer diffraction at a single slit. Show that the intensity of first subsidiary maximum is about 4.5% of the principal maximum.
8. **(a)** Newton's rings are observed normally incident in reflected light of wavelength 6000 Å. The diameter of the 10th dark ring is 0.50 cm. Find the radius of curvature of lens and thickness of the film. **(b)** Explain Michelson interferometer and derive expression for fringe shift due to displacement of mirror.

## ℹ️ 6. Important Notes

1. **SIRT ke mid-sem papers:** Ye college-internal hote hain (SIRT faculty khud banati hai), online publicly available nahi hote. Mid-sem ke liye apne seniors ya department se previous mid-sem papers maango. **University end-sem (jo upar diya hai) ke liye ye PYQs hi best source hain.**
2. **"med" agar Hindi medium ka matlab hai:** RGPV ke papers bilingual hote hain (English + Hindi dono side-by-side PDF mein printed), aur original PDFs mein Hindi translation bhi hota hai. Answer English ya Hindi dono mein de sakte ho.
3. **2021 ke papers** COVID ke chalte available nahi hain; 2020 se pehle ke papers purane scheme (BE-xxx codes) ke hain — pattern same tha, syllabus 90% same hai.
4. **Syllabus change check:** BT-201/102/203/204/205 ye codes Grading System (GS) ke hain aur 2022 se ab tak stable hain — purane papers bilkul relevant hain practice ke liye.
5. Har paper mein **8 mein se sirf 5 questions** karne hote hain — matlab har unit ke 1-1 question, aur options milte hain. Isliye har subject ke **kam se kam 6-7 units mein se 5 strong banao**.
6. Physics/Maths/BCEM mein **numericals** pakke aate hain (Newton's rings, fiber NA, RL calculation, truss forces, eigenvectors) — inke numerical practice karo, sirf theory ratne se kaam nahi chalega.
