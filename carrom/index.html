How to play

Standard carrom rules, simplified for a clean local match.

The board

9 white men, 9 black men and 1 red queen. Player 1 = white, Player 2 = black. Flick the striker from the baseline at your end to pot your own colour.

Scoring

  • Your own carrom man = +1 and you shoot again.
  • Opponent's man = opponent +1, and your turn ends.
  • Queen (red) = +3, but only when covered: pot it together with one of your own men, or use the one extra shot it gives you to cover it. Fail and the queen returns to the centre.
  • First to the target score wins. If all coins leave the board earlier, the higher score wins. Equal scores = draw.

Fouls & dues

  • Striker potted = foul. Every coin potted in that stroke is returned to the board and you owe 1 due.
  • A due is cleared by your next potted own man — that coin is returned instead of scoring.

Controls

  • Drag back anywhere on the board to set direction and power, then release to shoot.
  • Sliders set power, aim, spin (cut shots) and striker position on the baseline.
  • Keyboard: ←/→ striker · A/D aim · W/S power · Q/E spin · Space shoot · U undo · M mute · Esc close.

Physics

Real-time rigid-disc simulation: impulse-based collisions with restitution and Coulomb friction, spin transfer, rolling resistance, adaptive sub-stepping against tunnelling, and solid cushions with pocket jaws. Table polish and cushion bounce are tunable above.
